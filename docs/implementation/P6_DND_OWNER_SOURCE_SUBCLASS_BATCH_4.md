# P6 DND Owner-Source Subclass Batch 4

## Scope

The local DND 2024 owner source now supplies display-level subclass facts for:

- Rogue: Thief, Assassin, Arcane Trickster, and Soulknife.
- Sorcerer: Draconic Sorcery, Wild Magic Sorcery, Aberrant Sorcery, and
  Clockwork Sorcery.

The pre-existing `奥法诡术师`, `龙族血脉`, and `狂野魔法` display names are
retained for saved-character compatibility. The local source names those
entries `诡术师`, `龙族术法`, and `狂野术法` respectively. The existing XGtE
`风暴术士` entry remains outside this DND 2024 source batch.

## Boundary

This is selection and level-up preview data only. It does not automate spell
preparation or slots, Psionic Energy Dice, Sorcery Points, Wild Magic Surge,
telepathy, teleportation, damage, conditions, summons, targeting, reactions,
or Room Runtime authority.
